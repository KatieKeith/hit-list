# Hit List

Interval workouts built to the length you ask for. A single HTML page: no
accounts, no server, no tracking. Everything runs in your browser and your
settings and history are stored only on your own device.

**Open it:** https://katiekeith.github.io/hit-list/

On iPhone, open that link in Safari and tap Share then Add to Home Screen to
get it as a full-screen app.

## What it does

- Builds a warm-up, a circuit and a cool-down that add up to the time you ask for
- Strength and HIIT, or full-body flexibility
- Set the work interval, the rest interval and how many exercises per circuit
- Weight each muscle group from Off to High
- 140 exercises across bodyweight, dumbbells and a band, plus your own additions
- Leaves a muscle group alone for 48 hours after you train it, and avoids
  anything used in your last three sessions
- Makes sure impact and loaded work appear each week
- Spoken exercise names, countdown beeps, and it holds the screen awake
